Register the business with Gov...
1. To register as "Principal Entity Registration" on a DLT platform like JIO:
---- Need any of these orgaization type: Sole Proprietorship/Private Limited Company/ Partnership Firm
---- PAN/TAN & GST Number are required
---- Finalize a Business address

2. To get Business PAN/TAN
---- Register on https://www.proteantech.in/articles/business-pan-card-guide/
---- File Form 49A for a business PAN. File Form 49B to get a business TAN.

3. To get GSTN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
---- Once you have your business PAN, you can apply for your 15-digit GSTIN on Official GST portal.
---- Required: PAN, business details (like incorporation documents), and address proof to generate a Temporary Reference Number (TRN)

4. To register on MCA portal
---- MCA registration (like the SPICe+ form) is reserved only for structured entities like One Person Companies (OPCs), LLPs, or Private Limited companies, sole proprietors should follow these alternatives to establish a formal business.
---- PAN card, Identity proof, Address Proof, Digital Signature Certificate(DSC), Business Address Proof with NOC and Lease/Rent agreement if needed.
---- e-MOA (Memorandum of Association): Outlines the objectives of your aggregator business (connecting customers with laundry services).
---- e-AOA (Articles of Association): Lays out the internal rules and regulations of your company.
---- Director Declarations: E-forms DIR-2 and INC-9, which serve as declarations and consent to act as a director.
---- AGILE-PRO-S Form: This form is filed simultaneously to obtain your company’s PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC registrations, and optionally a state-specific professional tax and bank account.

Guide on registering My Company Online with MCA portal --> https://www.company-registration.in/blog/register-my-company-name-online-with-MCA-portal-complete-guide.php

Decide on registering as a "Sole Proprietorship" or "One Person Company(OPC)" ---> https://cleartax.in/s/one-person-company-vs-sole-proprietorship
---- If your business is a Sole Proprietorship, you do not need a separate business PAN. You simply use your personal PAN for business purposes. But, a separate business PAN may be required for GST registration and business banking.
---- For Sole Proprietorships, DLT portals allow registration without a GSTIN. What is LOA & why required for DLT registration:
A Letter of Authorization (LOA) for DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) is a mandatory document required by telecom operators to verify that a designated person can legally represent your business for SMS and voice communication registrations
